Why This Matters

3121 Individuals experience homelessness right here in Calgary. Across Canada, the number of unhoused people has nearly doubled over the past six years.

About 7% of Calgary's homeless population are youth between the ages of 12 and 24. In 2023 alone, 436 people experiencing homelessness died in Calgary, nearly double the number from the year before.
