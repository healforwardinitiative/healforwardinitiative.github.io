Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 15, 2026. Last updated: November 2026.

Who We Are

Heal Forward Initiative is a community-driven initiative based in downtown Calgary, Alberta. We are not a registered charity or incorporated nonprofit. We are a youth-led group dedicated to supporting people experiencing homelessness in Calgary. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Our contact for privacy matters is: info@healforwardinitiative.org.

What Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal information when you use our website:

  • Your name and email address (if you contact us).
  • Payment information (collected and processed securely by Zeffy and its payment processor, Stripe; we do not store your card details).
  • Basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, which our website host (GitHub) and our font provider (Google Fonts) may receive automatically when you load the site.

Why We Collect It

We collect your information to:

  • Process donations you make through our website.
  • Respond to messages or inquiries you send us.
  • Keep our website secure and working properly.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only for the purposes listed above. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Your information may be shared with:

  • Zeffy and Stripe, which process donations on our behalf.
  • Web3Forms, which delivers messages sent through our contact form to our team.
  • GitHub, which hosts our website, and Google Fonts, which provides the fonts used on our site.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website does not use cookies for analytics or advertising, and we do not currently run any analytics or advertising trackers. However, some third-party services used on our site, such as the Zeffy donation form and its payment processor, may set their own cookies. By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of those cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some features of the site, including the donation form.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Donation records may be kept for up to 7 years for financial accountability purposes.

Your Rights

Under Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request that we correct any inaccurate information.
  • Request that we delete your information, subject to any legal obligations.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@healforwardinitiative.org. We will respond within 30 days.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
