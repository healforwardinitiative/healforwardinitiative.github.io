Cookie Notice

Effective Date: April 15, 2026. Last updated: November 2026.

This Cookie Notice explains how Heal Forward Initiative uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website recognize your device and remember certain information about your visit.

How We Use Cookies

Our website does not use cookies for analytics or advertising, and we do not currently use analytics tools. Some parts of the site load content from third-party services that may use cookies or similar technologies in order to work properly:

  • Donation form: Zeffy and its payment processor, Stripe, may use cookies to process your donation, prevent fraud, and keep the form working.
  • Contact form: Web3Forms receives the details you submit so that it can deliver your message to our team.
  • Fonts: our site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when the fonts are requested.

Third-Party Cookies

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

Your Choices

By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this notice. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our Site. Most browsers allow you to: view the cookies stored on your device, delete cookies, and block cookies from being placed. Refer to your browser’s help section for specific instructions.
